# KIR virality criteria

Edit this file to tune what the clipper looks for. It is sent to the model as-is
every time a stream is analysed. Keep it plain English.

## Who the audience is
Keepingit_Rael (KIR) streams about self-improvement, life coaching, relationships,
and business. Viewers want real talk, practical advice, and moments that make them
feel something or want to share with a friend.

## What makes a great clip (score high)
1. **Strong first second.** The opening line grabs attention on its own: a bold claim,
   a question, a surprising number, a confession, or a direct "you" statement.
2. **Self-contained.** Someone with zero context understands it. No "like I said earlier".
3. **Hook, build, payoff.** It sets something up and lands it. The ending completes the thought.
4. **Emotional intensity.** Passion, humour, vulnerability, frustration, a story with stakes.
5. **Quotable or controversial.** A line people would screenshot, stitch, or argue about.
6. **Practical value.** A clear tip, framework, or mindset shift the viewer can use today.
7. **Tight.** 30 to 60 seconds is ideal. Every sentence earns its place.
8. **On-brand topic.** Self-improvement, discipline, money, business, dating and relationships,
   accountability, confidence, mental toughness.

## What to avoid (score low or skip)
- Stream housekeeping: "let me check chat", tech issues, intros, outros, ad reads.
- Reading chat names, shout-outs, raids, or anything that only makes sense live.
- Moments that need visuals the viewer cannot see (gameplay, screen shares).
- Rambling with no clear point, or a point that never lands.
- Anything that names or mocks a private person, or shares someone's personal details.

## Scoring scale (0-99)
- 90-99: Would stop a scroll. Clear hook in the first line, strong payoff, highly shareable.
- 75-89: Good clip, solid value, hook or payoff slightly weaker.
- 60-74: Usable but ordinary. Fine filler.
- below 60: Not worth posting.
Use the full range. Most moments in a stream are NOT 90+; reserve it for the best few.
